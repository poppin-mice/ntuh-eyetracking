 NTUH Eye-Tracking Suite - Release Notes

This release builds on the 2026-08-23 build (same three programs:
VA_center_opt.exe, calibration.exe, replayer.exe). How to run, where data is
saved, and the per-participant workflow are UNCHANGED - see the earlier release
notes in this folder for those. Below are only the changes since 2026-08-23.

Please include the version shown in the title when reporting an issue.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 CATCH TRIALS: SMARTER PLACEMENT  (VA_center_opt)  -- v1.5.0
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Negative Sample Collection Mode (General tab, "Collect negative samples:
N") keeps the same control and the same saved settings, but two things about
it changed:

  - N now means "N negative samples wanted", not "N catch trials". A normal
    trial the subject FAILS is already a negative sample, so real fails count
    toward N and only the remainder is filled with catch trials. A subject who
    keeps failing therefore gets few or no catch trials, instead of a block of
    them tacked on at the end.

  - Catch trials are no longer drawn from a fixed set of early slots. In the
    first field session (A01, 2026-08-25) both catch trials came AFTER the
    staircase had finished, as trials 10 and 11, because a subject who passes
    everything finishes in 9 trials and the old slots reached to trial 13.
    Now each trial is decided as it comes: trials 1 and 2 are always normal, a
    failed trial is always followed by a normal one, and otherwise the chance
    that the next trial is a catch tracks how many are still owed versus how
    many normal trials the staircase still needs. In practice: the catch trials
    spread over the whole test, the subject cannot learn a pattern (no fixed
    "real, blank, real, blank" alternation), two catch trials can occasionally
    come back to back, and for a subject who keeps passing they always fit
    before the test ends. This was chosen by simulation against the old scheme
    and two simpler fixed-probability variants.

Suggested N: 6 for adults, 3-4 for young children. A session gains roughly
7 seconds per catch trial.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 CATCH TRIALS VISIBLE IN THE REPLAYER  (replayer)  -- v1.2.0
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before this build the replayer listed a catch trial like any other, so a
reviewer could mistake its chance PASS/FAIL for an acuity result. Now:

  - the trial row shows a CATCH tag on a violet background (hover for a
    reminder that its pass/fail does not reflect acuity);
  - the timeline marker of a catch trial has a violet dashed outline over its
    usual pass/fail/discard colour;
  - the Trials group title counts them, e.g. "Trials (9 normal + 2 catch)";
  - review_labels.json gains "trial_type": "normal" | "catch" per trial, so the
    training pipeline can treat catch trials (negatives by construction)
    separately from the reviewer's pass/fail label.

Labels still pre-fill from the test's result and the 1/2/3 keys work as
before. Sessions recorded before catch trials existed show every trial as
normal. cpd values in the trial list are shown to one decimal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The catch-trial mode is for data-collection sessions; leave it OFF for
  ordinary clinical VA screening.
- The mode still needs a display fine enough to draw a grating above 20 cpd
  (4K or 3200x2000 at 50 cm is fine; 1080p is not) - it refuses to run and
  says so on the console otherwise.
- Keep the Chronus phone app up to date (this build's Sol SDK needs Chronus
  2.2.1 or newer, unchanged from the previous build).
- Detailed per-program documentation: VA_center_opt.md, calibration.md,
  replayer.md (in this folder).
